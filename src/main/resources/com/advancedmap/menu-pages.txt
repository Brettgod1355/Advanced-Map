= HELP

## Moving around
Drag to move, scroll to zoom around the pointer. The map opens on you, on the surface or the underground map, whichever you are on. The round button at the bottom right brings you back into view and keeps you there as you move (lit blue) until you drag the map. When you are out of view, an arrow at the edge points to you; click it to go back. After a jump (into a dungeon, to a search result or a pin), your mouse's back button returns to where the map was, and its forward button goes on again.

## Search
Click the box at the top and type: a place, a dungeon, anything a shop sells or a monster. Places come first, the bigger first ("var": Varlamore, Varrock); nicknames work ("ardy", "fally"); loose spelling too ("d scim", "kbd"). Up, Down and Enter pick a result, or click one. A place moves the map there; an item lists the shops that sell it, nearest first, and where it lies on the ground; a monster marks every spawn and lists them by area, under its hitpoints, Slayer level and masters, drops and a Wiki button. Fairy ring codes ("cks"), fishing spots ("lobster"), hunter areas and kinds of icon ("bank": every one, nearest first) are found too. The three lines beside the magnifying glass choose what the search looks in. Shops by name only with the setting.

## Markers
Right-click anywhere and choose "Add marker here": name it, pick a picture and a color, Save. Right-click a marker to edit or remove it. Markers show on all your accounts (a setting keeps them apart). Other plugins' markers (a clue scroll's spot, Quest Helper's next step) show too; click one for what it says. "Your markers" in the menu lists them all, nearest first.

## Farming, multi-combat, Slayer
A dot on each farming patch icon says how it is doing (from RuneLite's Time Tracking plugin); click it for each crop and when it is ready. "Multi-combat areas" at the top of the Filters list tints them red when zoomed in. "Your Slayer task" there marks every spawn of your task's monsters, with how many are left.

## Surface and underground
The button at the top right switches between the two maps. Basements, caves and dungeons are on the underground map.

## Floors
The arrows at the bottom right go up and down a floor. The floors below are shown darkened underneath, so you can see where you are. Zoomed out, the ground floor shows every floor's icons; zoomed in, each icon is on its own floor (Lumbridge Castle's bank is at the top). How floors are numbered is a setting.

## Dungeons
Point at a dungeon's entrance for a look inside: its name, a small map of where you come out and every monster inside, on its lower floors too (the Kalphite Queen, a rope down), the highest level first (a setting turns it round). Click the entrance to go in.

## Icons
Point at an icon, or click it to keep its box open, for its details: a quest's difficulty and the levels it needs, a shortcut's Agility level, a spell's Magic level. Shortcuts and teleports you can't use yet are grayed out with a red mark, and so are bank chests you haven't built yet and banks a quest hasn't opened for you; point at one for what it needs. Right-click an icon to hide it or open its wiki page. "Icons" at the top left turns every icon off or on at once; place names stay.

## Shops and transports
Click a shop for what it sells and for how much, in a list on the right. Click a spirit tree, fairy ring, charter ship or other transport for lines to where it goes and a list of the places with each fare and what the trip needs; click a place to go there.

## Filters
"Filters" at the top left drops down a list of every kind of icon with its picture and name, grouped (Banks, Quests, Shops ...). Tick or untick a whole group or a single kind, or type to search; the mouse wheel scrolls it. An orange dot on the button means some icons are filtered out.

## Hidden icons
Right-click one icon and choose Hide to hide just that one. "Show hidden icons" at the top of the Filters list shows them again, faded, so you can right-click one and choose Unhide. "Hidden icons" in the menu lists them all; click one to go there.

## Route here
Right-click anywhere, or on an icon, and choose "Route here" to have the Shortest Path or GPS plugin show the way. Choose which in the settings; it needs that plugin installed.

## Wilderness
Zoomed in, red dashed lines show where the Wilderness passes level 20 and level 30, above which most teleports stop working.

## Something wrong?
Right-click the spot, or the icon, and choose "Report a problem here": a new GitHub issue opens in your browser with the place filled in, and a picture of the map with the spot ringed is on your clipboard to paste in (Ctrl+V); say what is wrong or missing. Or write on Discord (the menu's "Help improve the map").

## Over the game
The map opens in a box over the game; the game goes on around it, and a click outside the box is the game's. Drag the bar along its top or bottom (the dots) to move it, a corner or side to size it. The square button at the top right fills the game view with it; press it again to go back.

## Pop out
The pop out button at the top right (a window with an arrow leaving it) moves the map into a window of its own; the same button there brings it back. Escape closes it in either.

## Menu
The three lines at the top left open the menu: a welcome (how far the map is built), this help, lists of your markers and of the icons you hid, the plugin's settings, what's new in this version, where to send ideas and bug reports, and building the map again. Escape closes what is open, one step at a time.

= BUILDING

## Once, then almost instant
The first time, Advanced Map builds the map on this computer from the game data your client already has, and saves it (about 60 MB). This happens once: from then on the map opens almost instantly, on every character and RuneLite profile on this computer. Nothing is downloaded.

## How the map is built
In the background from the moment the client starts, one region after another: the ground near you first, then farther out, then the floors above. It uses spare processor time, so the game comes first, and takes about 5 to 10 minutes for the whole world. You can play and use the map meanwhile: places not built yet are blank or blurry, and their icons are missing from the map and the search. The sidebar tab shows how far along it is.

## Game updates and plugin updates
Game updates need nothing from you. The map, its place names and the game's own map icons come from your client, so when an update changes the world (a new island, a moved wall), the regions it changed are built again the first time you look at them, and new ones in the background. Shops and what they sell, prices, monsters with their stats and drops, items on the ground, and teleport and transport details come from the OSRS Wiki with the plugin: they change when the Plugin Hub updates Advanced Map, which RuneLite does by itself when it starts.

= IMPROVE
Ideas, suggestions and bug reports are welcome, on Discord or on GitHub, whichever you prefer. Both open in your browser.
If something on the map is wrong or missing, say where. The map itself comes from the game's own data and keeps up with it; shops, prices, monsters and items on the ground come from the OSRS Wiki and are updated with the plugin.
Right-click a spot or an icon on the map and choose Report a problem here: a GitHub issue opens with the place already filled in.

= REDRAW
Building the map again deletes the map saved on this computer and builds every region again from the game's data. What this client has already shown stays on the map until it restarts; everything else is built again as you look around.
